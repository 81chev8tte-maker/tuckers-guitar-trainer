# Public-domain music in Family Music Quest

Family Music Quest stores no downloaded MIDI files, recordings, tutorial transcriptions, or commercial sheet-music arrangements. Each bundled piece below is newly encoded and harmonized for the app from its public-domain underlying composition. Lyrics are not included.

## Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star — Beginner Arrangement

- Composer/attribution: traditional melody, commonly associated with the eighteenth-century French tune “Ah! vous dirai-je, maman”
- Historical source: [Library of Congress, 1879 notated music](https://www.loc.gov/item/2023832590/)
- Public-domain basis: the Library of Congress item states that its collection is public domain and free to use and reuse
- FMQ version: original beginner arrangement in C major with newly written bass support
- Lyrics: no
- Simplification: compact range, repeated melody, simple I–IV–V harmony

## Frère Jacques — Beginner Arrangement

- Composer/attribution: traditional French round
- Approximate period: documented in the nineteenth century
- Historical source: [Library of Congress catalogue search](https://catalog.loc.gov/vwebv/search?searchArg=Frere+Jacques&searchCode=GKEY%5E*&searchType=0)
- Public-domain basis: traditional melody documented well before modern copyright terms
- FMQ version: original beginner arrangement in C major with a new bass part
- Lyrics: no
- Simplification: melody presented as a single line rather than a sung canon

## Row, Row, Row Your Boat — Beginner Arrangement

- Composer/attribution: traditional American children’s round
- Approximate publication: nineteenth century (an earlier version appeared in 1852; the familiar tune was in print by 1881)
- Historical source: Library of Congress historical collections/catalogue
- Public-domain basis: nineteenth-century melody
- FMQ version: original beginner arrangement in C major with new accompaniment
- Lyrics: no
- Simplification: compact melodic range and slower rhythm

## Jingle Bells — Beginner Arrangement

- Composer: James Lord Pierpont
- Publication: 1857 as “The One Horse Open Sleigh”
- Historical source: [Library of Congress, Greatest Hits 1820–1860](https://www.loc.gov/collections/american-sheet-music-1820-to-1860/articles-and-essays/greatest-hits-1820-60-variety-music-cavalcade/1850-to-1860/)
- Public-domain basis: original composition published in 1857
- FMQ version: original simplified arrangement with new bass and harmony
- Lyrics: no
- Simplification: chorus melody only, comfortable C-position range

## Ode to Joy — Beginner Arrangement

- Composer: Ludwig van Beethoven
- Source work: Symphony No. 9 in D minor, Op. 125
- Composition/premiere: 1824
- Historical source: [Beethoven-Haus Bonn work catalogue](https://www.beethoven.de/en/work/view/5024208720412672/Symphony+No.+9+in+D+minor%2C+Op.+125)
- Public-domain basis: Beethoven died in 1827; the composition is public domain
- FMQ version: original simplified arrangement in C-position with new bass support
- Lyrics: no
- Simplification: opening theme only, transposed/simplified for beginners

This documentation concerns the underlying compositions and FMQ-authored arrangements. It is not a claim that unrelated modern editions, recordings, MIDI files, or arrangements are public domain.
