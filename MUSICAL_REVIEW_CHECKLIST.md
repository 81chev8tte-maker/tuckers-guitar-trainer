# Built-in Piano musical review

Automated tests verify data integrity, musical form labels, timing, polyphony, arrangements, and rights metadata. They cannot decide whether a composition sounds satisfying. Each changed piece still needs a human Listen First review on the target Chromebook.

For every Full Song or Concert, check:

- the intended melody or named theme is complete and recognizable
- no major section is missing or present only as duration padding
- rhythm sounds natural, including eighth notes, rests, and held notes
- accompaniment follows the melody's harmony
- phrase and measure boundaries fall at musical points
- hand position and fingering are reasonable for a beginner
- the ending resolves naturally rather than stopping mid-phrase
- Listen First performs the full arrangement without scoring
- Melody Practice supplies accompaniment and stays monophonic for microphone input
- MIDI Hands Together retains simultaneous notes
- pause, exit, reduced speed, and phrase practice work cleanly

Record subjective findings separately from automated results. Do not mark a piece musically approved merely because its tests pass.
