# Family Music Quest music sources

Family Music Quest bundles no downloaded MIDI files, modern tabs, commercial arrangements, or recordings. Every bundled arrangement is authored for this project from an underlying public-domain composition or melody. Lyrics are not bundled.

| Composition | Attribution and date | Public-domain basis / source consulted | FMQ arrangements |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ode to Joy | Ludwig van Beethoven, Symphony No. 9, Op. 125 (1824) | [Beethoven-Haus work record](https://www.beethoven.de/en/work/view/5024208720412672/Symphony+No.+9+in+D+minor%2C+Op.+125); Beethoven died in 1827 | Piano complete principal theme; Guitar complete theme in C, easy lead tab |
| Jingle Bells | James Lord Pierpont, published 1857 | [Library of Congress historical overview](https://www.loc.gov/collections/american-sheet-music-1820-to-1860/articles-and-essays/greatest-hits-1820-60-variety-music-cavalcade/1850-to-1860/) | Piano and Guitar complete-refrain arrangements |
| Amazing Grace | John Newton text (1772); traditional New Britain tune | [Library of Congress song history](https://www.loc.gov/item/ihas.200149085/) | Guitar first-verse melody; no lyrics |
| Auld Lang Syne | Traditional Scottish melody; nineteenth-century editions | [Library of Congress public-domain sheet music](https://www.loc.gov/item/2023841187/) | Guitar first-verse melody; no lyrics |
| In the Hall of the Mountain King | Edvard Grieg, *Peer Gynt* (1875–76) | [IMSLP work page](https://imslp.org/wiki/Peer_Gynt_Suite_No.1%2C_Op.46_(Grieg%2C_Edvard)); Grieg died in 1907 | Guitar beginner theme, newly transposed/fingered |
| Twinkle, Twinkle, Little Star | Traditional, eighteenth century | [Library of Congress 1879 score](https://www.loc.gov/item/2023832590/) | Piano complete beginner arrangement |
| Frère Jacques | Traditional French round | [Library of Congress catalog](https://catalog.loc.gov/vwebv/search?searchArg=Frere+Jacques&searchCode=GKEY%5E*&searchType=0) | Piano complete beginner arrangement |
| Row, Row, Row Your Boat | Traditional American round, in print by 1881 | [Library of Congress sheet music](https://www.loc.gov/item/2023807004/) | Piano complete beginner arrangement |

The six Piano originals are original Family Music Quest compositions. `PUBLIC_DOMAIN_MUSIC.md` remains the detailed Piano v2.6.2 review; this file is the shared Piano/Guitar registry from v2.6.3.
